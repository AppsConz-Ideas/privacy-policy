Privacy Policy

Country Puzzle AI · Last updated: August 22, 2026

Country Puzzle AI (“the App”) is an offline-first geography quiz game published by Appsconz. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App stores and how it is used.

1. Information we collect

The App does not require an account and does not collect your name, email, phone number, or location on our servers. Progress is saved only on your device, including:

  • Explorer name and profile photo
  • XP, level, quiz scores, streaks, and statistics
  • Favorite countries and viewed country profiles
  • Achievement progress and daily mission status
  • Appearance, visual theme, and sound preferences

2. How information is stored

Player data is stored locally on your device (for example, using Shared Preferences / local storage). It stays on your phone or tablet unless you uninstall the App, clear app data, or delete the App.

3. Purchases and subscriptions

If you buy Country Puzzle Pro (subscription or lifetime unlock), payment is processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Play Store). We do not receive or store your full payment card details. Purchase status may be restored on your device so premium features remain unlocked.

4. What we do not do

  • We do not sell personal information.
  • We do not run a cloud account system in this version of the App.
  • We do not require you to create a login.
  • Country facts in the App are educational reference material bundled with the App.

5. Sharing

If you use Share App, your device’s share sheet opens. What you send, and to whom, is controlled by you and the app you choose (Messages, WhatsApp, email, and so on).

6. Ratings and store links

If you rate the App or open store listings, your device may open the Apple App Store or Google Play Store. Those platforms have their own privacy policies.

7. Children’s privacy

The App is a general-audience educational game. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because data stays on device, a parent or guardian can remove it by clearing app data or uninstalling the App.

8. Data retention and deletion

You can reset local progress by clearing the App’s storage in your device settings, or by uninstalling the App. Uninstalling removes locally saved progress.

9. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App changes. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do.

10. Contact

For privacy questions about Country Puzzle AI, contact us at contact@appsconz.ca or through the App Store / Google Play listing for this App.
