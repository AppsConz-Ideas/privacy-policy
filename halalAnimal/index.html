Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 6, 2026 · Last updated: September 6, 2026

No personal data collected

This Privacy Policy explains how Halal Animals (“the App”), operated by AppsConz Inc. (“we”, “us”), treats information when you use the iOS app. The App is a children’s educational product for learning about animals and Halal / Haram in a kid-friendly way.

Halal Animals works offline. We do not require an account, we do not show ads, and we do not use analytics or tracking SDKs.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect personal information. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, or payment details. We do not use IDFA, cookies, or advertising identifiers.

The App only stores information on your device so the App can remember your choices:

  • Favorite animals
  • Sound, haptics, theme, look, and similar settings
  • Quiz, daily challenge, memory, and rain-game progress or scores
  • Achievements and which animals you have opened

This information stays in Apple’s on-device storage (UserDefaults) on your iPhone or iPad. It is not sent to our servers. We do not operate a backend for this App.

2. How this on-device information is used

On-device data is used only to:

  • Keep your settings and favorites after you close the App
  • Show scores, progress, and achievements
  • Avoid repeating the same featured animal too often

We do not sell, rent, share, or use this information for advertising or profiling.

3. Children

The App is designed for children and families. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. We do not include social features, chat, accounts, third-party ads, or third-party analytics.

Parents and guardians can delete local App data at any time with Settings → Reset saved progress, or by deleting the App from the device.

4. Internet and third-party links

Gameplay, animal pages, quizzes, and settings do not require an internet connection. Some Islamic reference notes may include optional links (for example to quran.com) that open in Safari if you tap them. Those websites have their own privacy policies. We do not control and are not responsible for third-party sites.

5. Apple and App Store

Apple may collect information as described in Apple’s Privacy Policy when you download the App from the App Store. That processing is controlled by Apple, not by us.

6. Data retention and deletion

Because we do not collect personal data on our servers, there is nothing for us to delete from a cloud account. Data on your device remains until you reset progress in Settings or uninstall the App.

7. Security

We do not transmit your App data to our own servers. Information stored on your device is protected by the security of iOS and your device passcode or biometrics.

8. International users

The App does not transfer personal data from your device to us. If you use optional third-party links, those sites may process data according to their own policies and locations.

9. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy if the App’s practices change. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. If we ever start collecting personal data, we will update this policy and the App Store privacy labels before that happens.
